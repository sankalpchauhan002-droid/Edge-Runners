How to Play

  • Move: A/D, Left/Right Arrows, or Swipe
  • Jump: W, Up Arrow, Space, or Swipe Up
  • Slide: S, Down Arrow, or Swipe Down
  • Store: Press [1], [2], [3] or tap store buttons to use powerups.
  • Goal: Dodge obstacles, collect coins, and survive!
